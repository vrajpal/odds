The contest in one breath

Pick one NFL team to win straight up each leg. Wrong (loss or tie) = the entry is dead. Miss a deadline = dead. Each team usable once across all 20 legs. Last entry standing takes the whole $20M pool.

Mechanics

20 legsNFL Weeks 1–18 plus two standalone holiday legs: Thanksgiving (Wed Nov 25 – Fri Nov 27 games) and Christmas (Dec 24–25 games). Surviving the season means going 20-0.
deadlineSaturday 4:00 PM PT for normal legs. Thanksgiving leg: Wed Nov 25, 4 PM. Christmas leg: Thu Dec 24, 4 PM. Weeks 12 and 16 are short fragments after the holiday legs — their windows open Saturday 12 AM and close the same day at 4 PM.
⏱ early kickoffYou can't pick a team whose game already started, so a Thursday-night team's pick is due at kickoff. Unlike the Million there's no card to drag along — only the picked team's kickoff matters.
🦃 / 🎄 slatesThe holiday legs only offer the teams scheduled those days (10 at Thanksgiving, 8 at Christmas — PHI, GB, CHI, BUF, DEN, LAR appear on both). Arrive with your whole slate burned and you're eliminated with no legal pick (Rules 8/9). The Plan tab tracks this.
tie = lossRule 6a. There is no push in survivor. Enter ties as losses when grading.
missed leg = deadUnlike the Million (score 0, keep playing), a survivor entry that doesn't submit by the deadline is eliminated (Rule 12).
win %Straight-up win probability implied by the market spread (normal margin model, σ≈13.45). A -7 favorite ≈ 70%. Survivor is about not losing, but the used-team constraint means the "safest" pick each week is rarely optimal for the season.
ETSNCirca's 12-digit ticket serial from the app confirmation — the only proof the pick got in. Record it on the Pick tab.
repeat pick = DQRule 15a: if Circa's software lets a used team through, the entry is disqualified anyway — tracking used teams is on us. This app hard-blocks repeats.

Group workflow

blind proposalEach member privately backs one team; reveal after all (or your own) submission. Stops anchoring.
stance / voteAfter the reveal you can move your backing to any candidate. One stance per member; unanimous → lock it, majority → presumptive, contested → captain decides.
captainRotates per leg in member order; breaks deadlocks and is the submitter of record at Circa.
