Lines & numbers

spreadThe home team's line everywhere in this app. Negative = home favored: -3.5 means home must win by 4+ to cover; the away side of the same number is +3.5.
marketConsensus of the live sportsbook spreads we collect (median across books) — what the betting market currently thinks the line should be.
circa lineCirca's static contest spread, posted Thursday ~10 AM PT (Wednesday on Thanksgiving/Christmas weeks) and never updated. Entered here by hand — it exists on no data feed. It's the number all five picks are graded against.
modelOur market-implied power-rating prediction: team ratings + home-field advantage solved from every posted spread at once. A third reference — when it disagrees with a game's own line, one of them is off.
edgeCirca line minus market consensus, from the taken side's view. +2.0 = the contest number gives your side 2 points more than the market says is fair. The core value signal.
valueWhich side (home/away) the edge favors at the contest number.
keysKey numbers 3 and 7 — the most common NFL margins of victory. A contest line and market on opposite sides of one is worth more than the raw point gap (e.g. -2.5 vs -3.5 straddles 3).
driftHow far market consensus has moved since the Circa line was entered. Big drift toward your side = the market agrees with your pick more than when Circa set the number. Click a matchup for the full movement chart.
CLVClosing Line Value: the number you took vs where the market closed at kickoff. Positive CLV means you beat the market — the best evidence a picking process works, long before win/loss records mean anything (90 picks/season is a tiny sample).
HFAHome-field advantage in points, fitted from the market (~1.5–2 in the modern NFL).

Contest mechanics

contest weekWednesday 2:00 AM PT through the next Wednesday 1:59 AM PT (Rule 19). Weeks 1–18.
deadlineSaturday 4:00 PM PT — when the week's five picks are due at Circa (Rule 7).
⏱ earlyThis game kicks off before the Saturday deadline (Thursday night, December Saturdays). Rule 8: put it on your card and the whole card is due before that kickoff — no partial submissions.
booby prizeCirca pays last place $100k (2nd-last $50k; $25k per quarter) — but only to entries that made 5 picks in every completed week. Miss one week and you're permanently ineligible, which is why…
booby guard…the red header alert fires if no card is locked by Saturday 10 AM PT. A missed week costs the week's points and booby eligibility forever.
quarters / MVPThe season splits into Q1 (wks 1–4), Q2 (5–9), Q3 (10–13), Q4 (14–18); each quarter pays its own $150k first prize ("Quarter MVP").
ETSNCirca's 12-digit electronic ticket serial number — proof the card was physically submitted at the sportsbook. Record it on the Card tab after submitting.
pushThe game lands exactly on the number (home wins by 3 on a -3 line). Worth half a point in the contest.

Group workflow

blind proposalsEveryone submits their picks before seeing anyone else's — one shot, no edits. Stops the first opinion from anchoring the group.
stanceYour current side on a game. Voting on a game you proposed changes your stance — one opinion per member per game.
unanimous / majority / contestedHow many members back a candidate: all three / two / fewer. Contested picks fall to the captain.
working cardThe top five candidates by backing — what would lock right now.
captainThe week's tiebreaker and submitter of record, rotating in member order (week 1 → first member, and so on).
DIVDivisional matchup — same division, twice-a-year familiarity; these historically play closer to the number.
H-short / A-shortHome/away team on a short week (4 days' rest or fewer, usually Thursday after a Sunday game).
H-bye / A-byeComing off an idle week (13+ days' rest).
rest H+N / A+NRest differential of 3+ days in that team's favor.
