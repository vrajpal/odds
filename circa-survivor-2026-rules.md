# Circa Survivor — 2026 Pro Football Contest Rules

Compiled 2026-08-09 from the official contest rules PDF (Rev. 6/19/2026), the
official contest page, and press coverage. Sources at the bottom. The official
rules PDF is the authoritative document; Circa can update it at
[circasports.com/survivor](https://www.circasports.com/survivor) and entrants
are bound by updates (Rule 30).

> Note: **Circa Survivor** ($1k entry, pick one straight-up winner per leg,
> $20M) is a different contest from **Circa Million VIII** (5 picks against the
> spread, $6M — see `circa-million-2026-rules.md`) and **Circa Grandissimo**
> ($100k-entry survivor, $4M). This file covers Circa Survivor only.

## Eligibility & registration

- 21+ with valid government photo ID; entrants and proxies alike. Entries subject to third-party verification.
- Must be a Club One member (free card at participating sportsbooks/players' clubs).
- Register and pay **in person** at: Golden Gate, the D Las Vegas, Circa Resort & Casino, Tuscany Suites & Casino, Silverton Casino Lodge, or Legends Bay Casino (Sparks).
- Payment: cash, gaming chips (Golden Gate / the D / Circa), credit card (+fee), casino wire, or cashier's check.
- Registration paperwork not accepted after **2:00 PM Sat Sep 12, 2026**.
- Circa may reject any alias; fallback is your Contest ID number in public standings.
- Circa employees and specified personnel of the affiliated properties may not enter or act as proxies. Persons on the NV Excluded Persons List are ineligible.
- Contest page perk: complimentary Circa Survivor hat at signup, while supplies last.

### The Week-1 rebuy rule (Rule 3a)

The 10-entry cap has one carve-out, tied to the opening **Wednesday Night
Football (Sep 9)** and **Thursday Night Football (Sep 10)** games:

- If your original entries **lost on the opening WNF and/or TNF game**, you may
  buy replacement entries — up to **20 additional** total — before the Sep 12
  2:00 PM registration deadline, but only as many as you lost.
- This chains: buy 10, play them all on WNF, lose → buy 10 more, play them all
  on TNF, lose → buy 10 more before Saturday's deadline. That's the 30-entry
  worst case the rules explicitly walk through.
- Rebuys are registered under a **different alias** from the originals, after a
  Circa team member verifies your active-entry count against your contest card.
- Hard limit: you cannot go **into the Saturday signup deadline with more than
  10 active entries** — that's disqualification without refund (Rule 3c).

## How to play

Each Contest Week, every entry picks **one NFL team to win its game outright**
— no point spread. Rules of the pick:

- Each team may be used **once per entry** for the entire contest (all 20 legs).
- A **tie grades as a loss** (Rule 6a) — the entry is eliminated.
- One submission per entry per week; **no voids or changes** after submission (Rule 18).
- Multiple entries may make contradictory picks; each entry is independent (Rule 10).
- Missing a leg's deadline **eliminates the entry** (unlike Circa Million, where a missed week just scores 0).

### Weekly cycle (all times Pacific)

1. **Wednesday 10:00 AM — selection window opens** for the Contest Week.
2. **Saturday 4:00 PM — selection deadline.** Per the contest page, a pick is
   due at the earlier of the deadline or that game's kickoff — you can't pick a
   team whose game already started. Because you only need one team, skipping
   the Thursday game keeps your full window open (no Circa-Million-style rule
   pulling the whole card forward).
3. **Sunday–Monday — games play out.** Wrong pick (loss or tie) = eliminated;
   correct pick advances.

A "Contest Week" runs Wednesday 2:00 AM → following Wednesday 1:59 AM, except
around the holiday legs (below).

### The two holiday legs — the defining 2026 wrinkle

Thanksgiving and Christmas are **standalone Contest Weeks**, which is how an
18-week season yields up to 20 picks. Surviving to the end means going **20-0**.

**Thanksgiving Eve/Day + Black Friday leg** — games of Wed Nov 25, Thu Nov 26,
and Fri Nov 27, 2026:

- Contest Week runs Tue Nov 24 2:00 AM → Fri Nov 27 11:59 PM.
- Selection window opens **Tuesday 10:00 AM**; deadline **4:00 PM Wednesday, Nov 25**.
- Eligible teams (per the rules PDF): **Green Bay, LA Rams, Chicago, Detroit,
  Philadelphia, Dallas, Kansas City, Buffalo, Denver, Pittsburgh** (10 teams).
- **Elimination trap (Rule 8):** an entry that has already used all 10 of those
  teams has no legal pick and is **eliminated** at the conclusion of the leg.
- The rest of NFL Week 12 (Sun/Mon games) is its own normal Contest Week: window
  opens 12:00 AM Sat Nov 28, deadline Sat 4:00 PM, week ends Tue Dec 1 1:59 AM.

**Christmas leg** — games of Thu Dec 24 and Fri Dec 25, 2026:

- Contest Week runs Tue Dec 22 2:00 AM → Fri Dec 25 11:59 PM.
- Deadline **4:00 PM Thursday, Dec 24**.
- Eligible teams (per the rules PDF): **Houston, Philadelphia, Green Bay,
  Chicago, Buffalo, Denver, LA Rams, Seattle** (8 teams).
- **Elimination trap (Rule 9):** all 8 already used → eliminated at the
  conclusion of the leg.
- The rest of that NFL week (Sat Dec 26 onward) is its own Contest Week: window
  opens 12:00 AM Sat Dec 26, ends Tue Dec 29 1:59 AM.

Strategy consequence: teams playing the holiday slates (especially the overlap
teams — Philadelphia, Green Bay, Chicago, Buffalo, Denver, and the LA Rams
appear in **both** legs) carry option value that ordinary teams don't; burning
them early can strand an entry with no legal holiday pick.

## Game handling & grading

- All games are action regardless of venue change (Rule 11).
- A game that deviates from its scheduled time/place must **complete within its
  Contest Week** (by 1:59 AM Wednesday) or every pick on it grades as a **loss
  and the entry is eliminated** — harsher than Circa Million, where the same
  situation is a push.
- Forfeits: if the NFL awards a W/L without the game being played (within the
  Contest Week), picks on the NFL-deemed winner advance; picks on the loser are
  eliminated (Rule 11a).
- Software safety valve is on **you** (Rule 15a): if the app or kiosk
  erroneously lets you pick a team you've already used, the entry is
  **disqualified** once that Contest Week ends — no exceptions. Track your own
  used-team list per entry.

## Submitting picks

- At any Circa Sports location, a kiosk (contest card required), or the Circa
  Sports mobile app — app picks require being **physically inside Nevada**.
- App/kiosk technical malfunctions **never extend the deadline** (Rule 15).
- Anti-spoofing (Rule 15b): viewing contest lines from out of state locks the
  account out of submissions for 30 minutes — a trap for out-of-state entrants
  coordinating with a proxy — and never extends the deadline.
- Receipt: physical kiosk ticket, or the app confirmation screen with its
  **12-digit ETSN** — the only official record. Screenshot the app confirmation.

## Proxies

- Up to **3 proxies** per entrant; proxies register in person with the same ID requirements.
- Circa doesn't endorse or mediate proxy arrangements; the entrant bears all responsibility for picks getting in.
- Proxies can submit picks but **cannot collect prizes** — only the Official Contestant can.
- Out-of-state players routinely use Nevada proxy services; proxies are on-site at Circa during signup season.

## Prize & end conditions ($20,000,000 guaranteed)

- Prize pool = all entry fees, **no rake**; Circa tops it up to $20M if entries
  fall short of 20,000, and every dollar above 20,000 entries is added on top
  (2026 expectation per Circa: 22,000–23,000 entries).
- Winner determination at the end of each Contest Week (Rule 19d):
  1. **More than one entry left, weeks remain** → contest continues.
  2. **Exactly one entry left** → that entry wins the whole pool immediately,
     no matter how early in the season this happens.
  3. **Zero entries left** (everyone alive picked wrong the same week) → pool
     splits equally among all entries that **submitted a pick in that final week**.
  4. **Multiple entries survive all 20 legs** → pool splits equally among
     entries that submitted a pick in the final Contest Week.
- Payouts start **January 16, 2027** regardless of when the contest actually
  ends (aligned with Circa Million VIII season payouts) — an early sole
  survivor waits. Paid in cash/check/chips at winner's election.

## Cancellation & force majeure

- Contest is **official after Week 8** of the NFL season. Season shorter than 8
  weeks → all entry fees refunded. Season cut short at ≥8 weeks → all remaining
  entries split the prize.
- NFL suspension: contest pauses; 90 days with no completed NFL game ends it
  (refund if before Week 8, split among remaining entries if after).
- Standard force-majeure discretion applies to pre-start disruption; Circa
  management's interpretation of the rules is final; fraud = disqualification
  without refund.

## Payouts, disputes, fine print

- 5-day written-dispute audit period after winners post; then results are final.
- Prizes may be shared via IRS Form 5754 (max 10 people, all present in person with ID).
- Prizes must be collected in person by the Official Contestant within 365 days
  at the sign-up casino; after that Circa mails a check to the address on file.
  Estate provisions exist if a contestant dies mid-contest.
- Hedging funded by casino credit pays out only in Circa chips/plaques, applied
  against the marker (Rule 29).
- Circa House Rules apply; entrants grant unrestricted marketing use of name/likeness.

## History & practical notes (press coverage)

- Growth: 2020 (year 1) $1M guarantee / 1,390 entries → 2024 $10M / 14,266 →
  2025 $15M guarantee / 18,718 entries ($18.718M pool) → 2026 $20M guarantee.
- The 2025 contest ended in a **5-way split** of $18.718M: six finalists reached
  Week 18; one lost on Cincinnati (a last-second field-goal loss), and the five
  survivors split roughly $3.74M each. One finalist famously locked in profit
  with a ~$700k moneyline hedge.
- Both end-of-contest split modes have happened in survivor contests at these
  stakes — mass same-week elimination and multiple 20-0 survivors — so the
  Rule 19d split conditions are not theoretical.
- 2026 total across Circa's three football contests is a record $30M guaranteed
  (Million $6M + Survivor $20M + Grandissimo $4M).
